Bio

BlackLenz Vizual Artz is the visual work of Zerin Scales, a Los Angeles-based photographer who has been behind the camera since 2018. His work is rooted in culture, truth, and the responsibility of documenting people with care.

Over the years, Zerin has photographed game show hosts, LA bands, actors, local businesses, and community-centered brands. His style blends professional portraiture with a grounded, documentary eye for real moments, real people, and real energy.

BlackLenz is about more than taking a clean photo. It is about honoring presence, showing people honestly, and creating images that feel connected to identity, community, and story.

Alongside BlackLenz, Zerin is also co-owner of Epic Portrait, a school photography business focused on modern, organized, and meaningful school portrait experiences.

Available for headshots, creative portraits, brand moments, business events, and community-centered gatherings.
